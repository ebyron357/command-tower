# Sales Department

Capabilities owned by this department:

- `skill.account-research` (skill) — Research accounts with org map, pains, and triggers.
- `skill.prospect-research` (skill) — Research prospects and prepare outreach angles.
- `skill.discovery-prep` (skill) — Prepare discovery agendas, questions, and hypotheses.
- `skill.meeting-prep` (skill) — Prepare meeting briefs with goals, risks, and asks.
- `skill.proposals` (skill) — Draft proposals with scope, value, pricing framing, risks.
- `skill.follow-up` (skill) — Write follow-ups that advance next steps without pressure spam.
- `skill.objection-handling` (skill) — Prepare objection responses with proof and questions.
- `skill.pipeline-review` (skill) — Review pipeline health, stages, risks, and forecast quality.
- `agent.sales-strategist` (subagent) — Prepare account/sales strategy, discovery, and proposals.
- `cmd.sales-brief` (command) — Create an account/meeting sales brief.
