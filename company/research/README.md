# Research Department

Capabilities owned by this department:

- `cmd.competitor-brief` (command) — Produce a sourced competitor brief.
- `skill.deep-research` (skill) — Conduct deep multi-source research with citation discipline.
- `skill.source-verification` (skill) — Verify claims against primary sources and note confidence.
- `skill.competitive-intelligence` (skill) — Build competitor landscapes with evidence and gaps.
- `skill.trend-analysis` (skill) — Analyze trends with time horizon and weak-signal discipline.
- `skill.evidence-synthesis` (skill) — Synthesize conflicting evidence into calibrated conclusions.
- `skill.regulatory-research` (skill) — Research regulations with jurisdiction and disclaimer.
- `skill.technology-scouting` (skill) — Scout technologies with fit, maturity, and risk.
- `skill.research-quality-control` (skill) — QA research outputs for sourcing and overclaiming.
- `agent.research-director` (subagent) — Lead deep research with source standards and synthesis.
- `agent.competitive-intelligence-analyst` (subagent) — Produce competitor briefs and battlecards with evidence.
- `cmd.research` (command) — Run deep research workflow with source verification.
- `cmd.source-verify` (command) — Verify claims and attach confidence levels.
