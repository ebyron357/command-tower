# Legal Compliance Department

Capabilities owned by this department:

- `skill.legal-issue-spotting` (skill) — Spot legal issues and escalation needs—not legal advice.
- `skill.contract-review-support` (skill) — Support contract review with clause checklist and questions.
- `skill.policy-analysis` (skill) — Analyze policies for gaps, conflicts, and controls.
- `skill.compliance-checklists` (skill) — Build compliance checklists with evidence mapping.
- `skill.regulatory-tracking` (skill) — Track regulatory changes with jurisdiction tags.
- `skill.evidence-organization` (skill) — Organize compliance/legal evidence with chain of custody notes.
- `agent.compliance-reviewer` (subagent) — Review for compliance gaps and escalate legal questions.
- `cmd.compliance-check` (command) — Run compliance checklist workflow with escalation notes.
