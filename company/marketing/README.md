# Marketing Department

Capabilities owned by this department:

- `skill.market-research` (skill) — Research markets, segments, and demand with sourced claims.
- `skill.positioning` (skill) — Craft positioning and messaging pillars with proof points.
- `skill.brand-strategy` (skill) — Define brand strategy, personality, and narrative.
- `skill.campaign-planning` (skill) — Plan campaigns with audience, channels, offers, KPIs.
- `skill.seo-content` (skill) — Plan SEO with intent, structure, and measurement.
- `skill.social-media` (skill) — Plan social content with channel norms and brand voice.
- `skill.email-marketing` (skill) — Write email sequences with goals, segments, and CTAs.
- `skill.copywriting` (skill) — Write persuasive, brand-aligned copy for surfaces.
- `skill.editorial-calendar` (skill) — Build editorial calendars with themes and owners.
- `skill.content-repurposing` (skill) — Repurpose long-form into channel-fit assets.
- `skill.creative-brief` (skill) — Write creative briefs with audience, message, constraints.
- `skill.content-performance` (skill) — Review content performance and recommend iterations.
- `agent.marketing-strategist` (subagent) — Lead market positioning, campaigns, and content strategy.
- `cmd.content-plan` (command) — Produce a content/campaign plan with owners and KPIs.
- `cmd.market-research` (command) — Run sourced market research workflow.
